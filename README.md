# Himalayan Packaging Hub

Create a modern, high-converting, fully responsive mobile-first B2B e-commerce and wholesale catalog web application for my packaging supply business named "Himalayan Eco Packaging", located in Roorkee, Uttarakhand.

### 1. Device Responsiveness & Layout Architecture (Critical Requirement)
- Fully adaptive and responsive across all screen sizes: Smartphones (iOS & Android, 360px–430px), Foldables, Tablets/iPads (portrait & landscape, 768px–1024px), Laptops (1366px–1440px), and Large Desktop Displays (1920px+).
- Mobile-First Optimization:
  * Sticky bottom navigation bar on mobile phones with quick-access tabs: "Home", "Catalog", "Search", "Quote Cart", and "WhatsApp".
  * Hamburger slide-over drawer menu on mobile/tablets with nested accordion dropdowns for all categories and subcategories.
  * Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 44x44px for buttons and selectors).
  * Product grid adapts dynamically: 1 column on small phones (<480px), 2 columns on larger phones/tablets (481px–768px), 3 columns on small laptops (769px–1200px), and 4 columns on large screens (>1200px).
  * Swipeable horizontal carousels with smooth touch-drag for featured categories, sample kits, and best-sellers on handheld devices.
- Desktop & Tablet Optimization:
  * Persistent top navigation bar with full dropdown mega-menu on desktop.
  * Collapsible left-side filter panel on desktop/laptop; opens as a slide-up bottom sheet or side drawer on mobile phones.
  * Right-side sliding slide-over "Quote Drawer" that renders cleanly without shifting background page elements.

### 6. Interactive Features & B2B Functionality
- Instant Category & Keyword Filter: Users can filter by "Eco Biodegradable", "Standard Plastic", "Boxes & Hampers", or search by keywords (e.g., "500ml", "Burger", "Saree").
- B2B "Add to Quote" System: Clicking "Add to Quote" adds products with customizable box quantities into a sliding Quote Drawer.
- One-Click WhatsApp Quote Exporter: In the Quote Drawer, clicking "Request Bulk Price on WhatsApp" generates a pre-formatted message containing:
  * Selected Product Names & Requested Box Counts
  * Delivery Location (Roorkee, Haridwar, Rishikesh, or other)
  * Directly opens WhatsApp with the business number pre-filled.
- Sample Kit Request Modal: A clean form where cafe owners or boutique managers can enter their Outlet Name, Location, and request a physical sample box.
- Wholesale FAQ Section: Delivery timelines (24-48 hrs), payment policy (100% Cash/UPI on Delivery), and custom logo printing inquiries.
